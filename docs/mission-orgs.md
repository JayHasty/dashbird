## What is IRENA?

**International Renewable Energy Agency** — intergovernmental org (UN observer, not a UN Secretariat department). ~172 members. HQ **Masdar City, Abu Dhabi**; also Bonn.

It is the dedicated **renewables knowledge/policy shop**: data, country advice, technology and finance guidance so governments actually deploy solar, wind, hydro, bioenergy, etc. Think “IEA, but only renewables, and more Global South deployment advice.”

**For Jay:** theme is on-brief (energy deployment + policy). **Hiring is Abu Dhabi/Bonn**, UN-style grades, and the official board is often empty. Treat as a **watch**, not a primary hunt.

Careers: https://www.irena.org/About/Employment/Vacancies-and-recruitment

---

## What is ESMAP?

**Energy Sector Management Assistance Program** — a **World Bank–administered, donor-funded** global program (not a separate employer). Partners include governments and foundations. Based with the Bank’s Energy & Extractives practice, mainly **Washington, DC**.

It funds **analysis + technical assistance** so countries can hit SDG7 (energy access) and decarbonize: electricity access, clean cooking, renewables, efficiency/cooling, data/analytics, coal-region transition. Bank operational teams then use ESMAP work to design real projects.

**Jobs are World Bank jobs** titled Energy Specialist / Senior Energy Specialist sitting on the ESMAP team. Grade **GG** (senior) can reach ~$180k net in DC; **GH** (lead) more clearly clears. Country-office posts (Dhaka, Colombo, etc.) fail the location rule.

Careers: World Bank Energy / ESMAP via https://www.worldbank.org/en/about/careers

## Scale AI — what you’d actually care about

Scale started as the **labeling/eval factory** for frontier labs. The interesting half now is **getting AI into governments and high-stakes ops**, not teaching GPT to chat.

**On-brief work**

- **DOE Genesis Mission** — consortium with the Energy Department and national labs: make DOE scientific data usable for AI, evaluation benchmarks, agents for science workflows. Energy + public-sector deployment, not a climate NGO.
- **Government deployments** — they write about operational AI (permitting, citizen services, legislative workflows) with audit trails and on-prem/sovereign constraints. Same “tech has to work for real institutions” motion as FDE/CE.
- **Donovan** — their gov/mission agent platform (alerting, crisis simulation, course-of-action). They explicitly mention **energy and water infrastructure** as a place those ops tools matter.
